Hey there, I'm Pranav Bagrecha 👋

I'm a first-year Computer Science student at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay — one of the most exciting places in the world to be an engineer. Every day here teaches me something new, whether it's from a lecture hall, a late-night hackathon, or a spirited debate in the hostel corridor.

My interests lie at the intersection of robotics, autonomous systems, and music technology. I love the idea of building machines that can sense, think, and act — whether that's a robot navigating a maze or a drone performing an aerial dance to a beat.

"The best way to predict the future is to invent it." — Alan Kay

When I'm not coding or studying, you'll find me strumming my guitar, experimenting with drone flight mechanics, reading about emerging tech, or binge-watching robot competitions on YouTube.
